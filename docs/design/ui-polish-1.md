# UI polish 1: the reader, the chat box, Home (5 Nov 2026)

Started from the owner's six pictures of the live site (HEAD `bea380c`) and his list of eight faults, and from one line of the chat performance gate that failed once in six runs. Each item below says what was wrong (the cause, found with a picture or a measurement and not guessed), what was changed, and what is left. Tests: `apps/web/e2e/polish-reader.mjs` and `apps/web/e2e/polish-chat.mjs` (both registered in `apps/api/scripts/integration.mjs`); each was written first and seen to fail on the build of `bea380c`.

## 1. The way back from a paper is a round arrow
`DocWorkspace.tsx`: the pill "My Workspace" is an `icon-btn` (44 px, round) with the arrow only, `aria-label` and `title` "Back to My Workspace". It does what it did. The header does not move once the paper is drawn (a layout-shift observer reads the `.dw-head` sources: 0). The paper's titles start 100 px further left, which is the point.

## 2 and 7. The drawing toolbar
**Cause of "Undo and Redo came below".** `ink.css` let the toolbar wrap by itself: at every width from 1600 down to 320 px the pair ended on the second row, and a divider (a separate flex item) was left alone at the end of the first row (the purple circle and the loose line of the owner's second picture are the last colour and that divider). The sizes and colours also sat on the first row beside the tools, so the second row began with colours.
**Now** (`InkToolbar.tsx`, `ink.css`): four parts that move as a whole (tools; size and colour; undo and redo; saved mark and close), each divider inside the part it belongs to, and three states stated in container queries (the paper's column, not the window):

| container width | rows |
|---|---|
| 812 px and more (a 1280 window with the side panel open: 825) | one: tools, size and colour, then undo, redo, saved mark, close at the end of the row, behind a divider |
| 600 to 811 | two: the tools; then size and colour, and at the end of that row the pair, the saved mark, close |
| under 600 (a phone) | three: tools; size and colour; the pair on the left and the saved mark and close on the right |

A test goes from 1600 to 320 px wide and checks that every button is inside the toolbar, no divider is alone, and the pair is at the end of the last row beside the close button. No scroll bar anywhere (`overflow` is hidden only for the opening animation). The tools and size buttons grow with the room up to 34 px and are never under 24 px (WCAG 2.2); under a container of 330 px (a window of 355) the eleven tools take two rows with no dividers. **Decided with evidence:** at two and three rows the pair is under the tools, because the DOM order is the Tab order and a pair on the first row would have been Tabbed after the sizes; the owner can ask for the pair on the first row of the two-row state (the cost is a Tab order that jumps from row to row).
**Look (7).** The chosen tool is a soft tinted rounded square with a hairline ring (the accent for its icon), not a filled circle. **Cause of the blobs of the owner's sixth picture:** `.inkbar-size span { background: currentColor; border-radius: 50% }` also matched the letter "A" of the Text tool's sizes, so each letter sat on a dark ellipse of its own colour. The dots are `.inkbar-dot` now: circles of whole pixels that keep the proportions of the real line widths (3, 6, 10 and 18 px for the pen; before: 4, 4, 4.19 and 7.69 px, two sizes the same and two drawn on a fraction of a pixel); the chosen size has a 2 px accent ring on a tint. Colours are a 24 to 28 px target round a dot of 18 px with a hairline, and the chosen one has a gap and a ring. Contrast, read in the browser in the three themes: the chosen tool's icon 6.08, 6.55 and 5.49 : 1 on its tint, the size ring 6.08, 6.55 and 5.49 : 1 on its tint, the tools that are not chosen at least 5.32 : 1 on the bar; axe has no finding in the three themes at 1280 and in dark at 390.

## 3. No scroll bar in a message box
**Cause.** Two. The question box of a paper was fitted when the panel was built: on a phone and in a window under 1000 px the Ask panel is a tab that is not shown, a box that is not shown measures 0, and the box was set to 0 px (the CSS minimum, 24 px) while its own line is 36 px: a scroll bar inside a one-line box (the owner's third picture). And every box past its largest size showed the browser's own bar with arrows (15 px on Windows).
**Now.** `lib/autogrow.ts`: `fitField` (does not measure a box that is not on the screen; adds a pixel when the whole-number `scrollHeight` is a fraction short) and `useGrowingField` (fits again when the width changes, when the fonts arrive, and when a sheet opens). Used by the paper's question box, the Home box, the editor of a message and the Ask sheet of an open-access paper. `shell.css`: `scrollbar-width: none` and `::-webkit-scrollbar { display: none }` for those four, and no resize grip. The box grows to its largest size and then scrolls by wheel, touch and keyboard (tested: wheel, Ctrl+End, Shift+Enter and Enter). **Gotcha (React error 185):** the Home box must be fitted after the paint (`useEffect`), not in a layout effect: with the fit in the commit the box changed its height in the same commit as the framer-motion layout measure of the form and typing a long text raised "Maximum update depth exceeded" in 2 of 4 runs of `polish-chat.mjs` part 3; with the effect 0 of 6 (`useGrowingField(..., after = true)`).
Not changed, on purpose: the form fields that are not message boxes (Idea Check's text, the reference list, the plan editor, notes, the literature scan).

## 4. The edge of the Home box
**Cause (a picture: the same page with the two rules switched off, `scratchpad/ui1/pairs/04-edge-zoom-*.png`).** `.hm[data-chat] .hm-box::before` and `::after` (the fade above and the strip under) are children of the box, and a child is painted over its parent's own border and shadow: the strip started at the padding edge, 1 px inside the border, so it covered the bottom border, the lower half of the 4 px focus ring and the shadow (the bottom row of the border read the page's colour, 251,250,247, against 53,72,200), and the fade covered the top ring. **Now** (`shell.css`): the two pieces of the page's colour are two more layers of the box's own `box-shadow`, under its ring and its shadow (`0 -1rem .5rem -.5rem var(--bg)` and `0 .55rem 0 0 var(--bg)`); the ring layer is there, empty, when the box is not focused so that both states have the same layers. The reason the fade was made stays (a black bar put under the box reads 0 far away and about half of the page's own brightness 8 px above its edge: 137 of 250 in light, 129 of 236 in read, 9 of 17 in dark); nothing is drawn over the box.

## 6. The arrow to the latest message
`ChatView.tsx` (`JumpToLatest`, `styles/chat-lazy.css`, which loads with the conversation's chunk: 541 bytes gzip, not in the first load). An empty element ends the conversation; an `IntersectionObserver` whose bottom edge is where the sticky box begins (made again by a `ResizeObserver` when the box grows) flips one piece of state when the end goes out of or comes into view: nothing else is drawn for a scroll. The button is drawn by a portal into an anchor on the box's top edge (`.hm-jump`, made by `HomePage`), 0.9 rem above it, centred, so it follows a box that grows. 40 px, round, `aria-label` "Scroll to latest message", in no live region, `visibility: hidden` (so out of the Tab order) when the end is in view. Smooth scroll, instant under reduced motion; pressed by the keyboard the focus goes to the box. Tested at 1280 and 390 in the three themes with a stored chat of 20 replies, a short chat (none), a box grown by six lines, the Tab order, reduced motion and a reply written while the reader is far up (no error 185).

## 8. Home starts a new chat
`App.tsx` `goHome` (the Home link, the logo, Home in the command palette; `Shell.tsx` gets `onHomeLink` and keeps `onHome` for "New chat" and the chats of the rail): on the empty Home with no chat it makes nothing (the box takes the focus, the page goes to the top, what was typed stays); from a conversation or any other page it makes `startNewChat()` and shows the Home (the old conversation stays in the list). It asks first (`lib/leave.ts`) when something would be lost that is kept nowhere: a private chat with messages, a message edit whose words were changed. History: no entry is added when the page is already at the address; Back from the new Home goes to the page before; Back from Settings brings the conversation back (only the Home link starts a new one). **The owner's to decide:** a private chat and an unsent edit ask with the browser's own confirm (as signing out with an unsent drawing does); an in-page dialog would be nicer and is more work.

## The shift gate (`chat-perf.mjs`, 320 px)
The line swung (0.000, 0.28 to 0.36, once 0.966). **Why**, from a probe that records every shift with its sources, every programmatic scroll and every key: (1) the gate types four letters while the reply is written, and in a long chat the first of them made the page jump by 2,000 to 46,000 px (115,998 once at 360 px) away from the reply: the form of the box has `layout="position"` and `layoutDependency` that changed for every message and every word, so for the first message after the page had scrolled to the new question it measured the box's place in the document and made it fly in from tens of thousands of pixels, and the page followed the caret into it. With the reply off the screen its shifts were not counted (0.000); when the jump came late, the old replies sliding into the window were (0.69 to 0.92), and a loaded machine moved everything in time. A real fault, not only a gate artefact: typing the next question while a reply is written, in a long chat. The measure depends on `framed` only now (the box glides when the page changes between the empty Home and a conversation, and not for every message). (2) What the first word really did: the answer's card was put in above the papers at the first word (0.196: the papers on the screen were pushed down 281 px at 320 px), and the "about 10 to 20 seconds" hint stood in the list of steps and went at the first word (the card and papers moved up by it). The card is there, empty and quiet (three grey bars and the hint), from the moment the papers are read (`step` read, the same moment as the papers, and only for a signed-in person whose papers have abstracts, which is exactly when an answer is attempted). Not changed: the steps list going when the answer is whole, and the card's height against the answer's (0.087), and an answer that does not come leaves the card away at the end. The gate now also fails when the page moves of itself after the first 300 ms (`lateJump`, 50 px), and the phone line is 0.35. **Values** of the 320 px line in ten runs in a row of `node e2e/chat-perf.mjs` on the final build (limit 0.35): 0.087, 0.087, 0.087, 0.087, 0.087, 0.087, 0.087, 0.087, 0.087, 0.165, and in every run the page moved of itself by 0 px; the 360 px line 0.071 to 0.153; the desktop's 0.003 to 0.035 (limit 0.1; the old 0.000 to 0.004 was measured with the page jumped away, 4,637 px in the run that was looked at). The same gate on `bea380c` fails three lines (the page moved 4,637, 3,573 and 115,998 px).

## Found on the way: React error 185 when a long text is typed in the Home box
`polish-chat.mjs` part 3 types sixteen lines into the Home box (the browser started with its scroll bars). On the build of `bea380c` it raised "Minified React error #185" (maximum update depth) in 2 of 6 runs; on the final build 0 of 9 (6 runs of the part alone, the whole suite twice and the integration run). The cause was not isolated: the box showed the browser's bar past 220 px (15 px of its width, so its text wrapped differently with and without it), and a first version of the new fit made in the commit itself (a layout effect) raised the error in 2 of 4 runs, which is why `useGrowingField(..., after = true)` fits the Home box after the paint. That did not remove it: **the cause was found on 5 Nov 2026** (the build of `5aa49ef` raised it in 2 of 4 full runs of the suite, in 4 of 5 runs of part 3 alone, and in 40 of 40 runs of a stress script that types sixteen lines at once). It was never the box's fit. `HomePage` called `setProblem(null)` in an effect on every key (`useEffect(() => setProblem(null), [query, tool])`). React skips a setter that is given the value the state already has only when the component has no work waiting, and the Home page (it reads several stores) usually has some, so every key's render ended with an update waiting. React counts the renders that end so and stops at fifty in a row (error 185); the count starts again whenever the scheduler gets a turn, which it does between keys typed by a hand and does not between fifty keys that come back to back (Playwright's keys on an unthrottled machine, a key held down after a long task, an input method). Slowing the processor four times hid it (0 of 40). The setter is now called only when there is a note to clear. The same line was in the command palette (`setActive(0)`), the search of a paper (`setFindIdx(0)`) and the search of the papers (`setState({ status: "loading" })` on every key), and is changed there too. `e2e/typing-burst.mjs` puts 120 input events into each of twelve boxes in ONE task (no race): on the old build seven of them fail every time.
