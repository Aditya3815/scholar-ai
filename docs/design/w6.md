# W6: the new look becomes the default (W6a, 27 Oct 2026)

Plan: phase 6 of [unified-workspace.md](unified-workspace.md): "polish, bundle and first-paint budget, docs, remove the flag". Done here as **W6a**: the new look is what everyone gets, with budgets and tests. **W6b** (taking the flag and the old pages out of the code) is separate, on purpose: see "What comes next".

## What changed for a person

- Anyone who opens the product now gets the new look: the intro page when signed out, the rail and the home screen when signed in. Someone who had turned it on with `?ws=1` before still has it.
- **A way back for one release:** `?ws=0` shows the old pages and is remembered in that browser; `?ws=1` returns. Nothing is on the server. (The plan said "remove the flag". I kept it for one release because the change is for every user at once and a per-browser way back costs nothing; a server-side switch would have meant new infrastructure.)
- Three things the old pages had that the new frame did not. They were found by running the old pages' own journeys on the new default, not by reading code:
  1. **Admin.** An admin reached `/admin` from the account menu, and the new rail had no entry. The rail now has "Admin" for admins (and the phone menu too).
  2. **Linking another way to sign in.** "Link ORCID" or "Link Google" existed only in the old account menu. It is now in Settings, under Account.
  3. **Web addresses.** Going to Admin, Privacy, Terms or "How we verify" from inside the app showed the page but left the address bar on `/`, so Back and reload were wrong. The address now follows the page.

## Tests

- New browser suite `e2e/default.mjs`: with nothing set a visitor gets the new look; every address a visitor can reach (`/verify`, `/idea`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/how-we-verify`) opens with a title and a heading and no sideways scroll; `/admin` is "Page not found" for a visitor and for an ordinary person; an ordinary person has no Admin entry; Settings offers "Link ORCID" (axe light and dark); checking references from the rail (current page, result, "Report a problem", axe); an admin has Admin in the rail, it opens inside the frame, axe light and dark, fits a phone, and the phone menu has it; Back, Forward and reload; `?ws=0` and `?ws=1`, including that the old stored value `1` still means the new look.
- Suites that tested the old chrome now run on the new look: `verify`, `papers`, `scan`, `idea`, `admin` and `library` (the Library and Settings pages replace the drawer and the dialog; "Open and ask" is the cover button). Only `auth.mjs` stays on the old pages (it is about the old account menu); it goes with them in W6b.
- Test-only fixes found on the way: the "Ask this paper" browser suite and the endpoint suite shared one slow paper, so in a full run the progress bar had nothing left to show (it also failed in the W4 full run); the browser suite now has its own (`oa.slow2`). The answer's fade-in is waited out before an axe scan.
- Unit tests 1,283 pass; typecheck clean; the full integration run with all the browser suites passes (one suite needed a wait for the lazy Idea Check chunk, fixed and rerun).

## Budgets

**Size** (`apps/web/scripts/budget.mjs`, `npm run budget -w apps/web`, a CI step after the build). It reads what the built `index.html` asks for at once and measures gzip, so it cannot drift from what is really downloaded; it also fails if a heavy chunk (the PDF reader, the intro page, `pdf-lib`) ever enters the first load.

| First load | Before (W5 build) | Now | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scripts, gzip | 249.5 KB | **224.4 KB** | 250 KB |
| Style sheet, gzip | 23.6 KB | 23.6 KB | 26 KB |
| Preloaded fonts (two files) | 166.6 KB | 166.6 KB | 185 KB |
| Intro chunk (loaded for signed-out visitors), script / style, gzip | 12.9 / 4.1 KB | 13.0 / 4.1 KB | 15 / 5 KB |
| PDF reader chunk (only when a paper opens), gzip | 59.9 KB | 60.0 KB | 66 KB |

The saving came from route splitting: Admin, the three legal pages, Check references and the Idea Check page and workspace are chunks fetched on arrival (a "Loading…" line of fixed height meanwhile, so the footer does not jump). **Tried and reverted:** splitting the home screen, Library and Settings too saved only 3.5 KB and made the footer jump (layout shift 0.2 on a phone). The paper pages are not split, so that they open offline once offline reading is on.

**First paint and layout shift** (`e2e/perf.mjs`, in the browser integration run). Edge with the processor 4 times slower and a 1.6 Mbit/s link with 150 ms delay, as in [scroll-site.md](scroll-site.md); paint times are read before any scrolling (the browser stops counting largest paints at the first input). Measured on a development machine:

| Screen | First contentful paint | Largest paint | Layout shift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intro, desktop | 3.5 s | 3.5 s (the headline) | 0.000 |
| Intro, phone | 3.4 s | 3.4 s | 0.000 |
| Home, desktop (not signed in) | 2.8 s | 2.8 s | **0.082** |
| Home, phone | 2.7 s | 2.7 s | 0.003 |
| Check references, desktop (deep link) | 2.5 s | 3.7 s | 0.024 |

Gates: layout shift at most 0.1 ("good"); paint times at most 4.5 s and 5 s (loose, because a shared CI machine is slower; they catch a real regression such as an eager chunk or a blocking font, not a 10% drift).

**Honest reading.**
- The plan's target was a largest paint under 2.5 s on this profile. The **intro misses it: 3.4 to 3.5 s** (it measured 2.6 s on 24 Oct). Since then the first-load script grew with the chat, the Ask-this-paper sheet, offline reading and Idea Check in the conversation. The remaining weight is the app's single main script (about 190 KB of the 224 KB) and the 122 KB serif font. Ways down, none done: a smaller or subset serif font (a change of typography, which is the owner's call), moving more of the app out of the main script (the home screen split did not pay), and measuring on the real CDN, where the files are compressed and cached; the local server used here is the pessimistic case.
- **Home, desktop: layout shift 0.082.** The serif headline arrives about 100 ms after the first paint on a slow link, and the box and cards below move once. It is under the 0.1 line, but it is close, and it was 0.000 before the script got smaller only because first paint was later. A metric-matched fallback font would fix it but depends on the optical-size axis of the font; not attempted.
- These are lab numbers on one machine, not field data.
