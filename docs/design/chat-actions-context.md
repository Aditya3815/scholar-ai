# Message actions and conversation context (research and plan, 2 Nov 2026)

**Status (3 Nov 2026): built and reviewed, not yet live.** Copy, Edit-and-resend, context in a chat and chat to chat are built together with the privacy text of section 7 (`PRIVACY_VERSION` `2026-11-draft-14`); nothing is pushed, so nothing of it runs on the live site until it is. **Section 11 says what was built differently from this plan** (the plan below is the design as decided on 2 Nov; where the two differ, section 11 and the code are right). Still open and the owner's: the allowance (D6) with the per-answer number (measured on a real model only after the first run of the follow-up eval), whether consent is asked before history is sent, a lawyer's read of the draft text, the DPDP dates, how a sign-in that only runs out is treated (section 11), and the ChatView and Trending work queued next ([roadmap.md](../roadmap.md), "Decisions waiting for Aditya"). Rollback of the context alone: the Worker variable `CHAT_CONTEXT=off` ([runbook.md](../runbook.md)); the browser has no switch of its own.

Asked by the owner on 2 Nov 2026: on the Home chat there is **no Copy and no Edit-and-resend** on a message, and the box has **no memory between messages** ("production grade, as ChatGPT and Claude do: the context of one message in the next message, and of one chat in another chat").
This note says what happens today, what seven read-only studies found (four web, three code; the full reports are in the session record, not the repository), what is decided, what is the owner's to decide, and the order of work. Method: [how-we-work.md](../how-we-work.md).

## 1. What happens today (read from the code)

- A conversation lives **only in the browser** (IndexedDB store `chats`, one record `{id, messages}` per chat, plus a localStorage index `{id, title, updatedAt}`, at most 30). The server keeps no chat.
- `POST /api/v1/chat` takes `{mode, message, scope, anyway}`. **Earlier messages are not sent.** "Only after 2020", "explain that simpler", "aur uske side effects?" are each a new search of those words (`chat-w2.md`, `router-w3.md` and `chat-w5.md` list it as not done; `unified-workspace.md` section 4.7 had planned "the last six messages").
- Worse than "not used": the **rules run before any model** (`ruleRoute`, `isSmallTalk`), and they settle follow-ups wrongly. Probed on the real rules: "only after 2020", "uske side effects?", "why?", "explain that simpler", "tell me more about paper 2", "aur bachon me?" all become a search of the raw words, no model, confidence 0.9; "what is that?", "really?", "are you sure", "so what" are taken for small talk and get the box's introduction. A follow-up written only in the model router would never run.
- Search has **no filters** (`runSearch(env, query, limit, ...)`): "only after 2020" cannot be a text query.
- The user bubble has no action at all. A reply has Search again (only on a failed or interrupted one), Change, Check strictly. Three hand-copied "Copy answer" buttons exist elsewhere (Ask this paper, the open-access Ask sheet, the preview); none on a question.
- A whole answer is about **9,000 tokens** of the **60,000** a day (D6, undecided), so an edit-and-resend costs about 15% of a day, and every token of history is taken from the same allowance.

## 2. What the usual practice says (and what could not be checked)

- **Per-message actions** (Open WebUI and LibreChat code read; ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Gemini only through community and press, because `help.openai.com` answers 403 to every fetch and the Claude help centre has no article on it): a small icon row; user message: Edit and Copy; reply: Copy, Regenerate, feedback. Revealed on hover **or keyboard focus** on older messages, always on the latest reply, and (LibreChat) always on touch devices (`@media (hover: hover) and not (any-pointer: coarse)`, because a hybrid laptop says "hover" while its touch screen cannot).
- **Edit** in the clients that read well: an inline editor, **Cancel** and one commit button; the old turn is **kept, not deleted**: a new sibling user message under the same parent, a "1/2" pager on the turn, and the model gets **only the active path** (root to leaf). The Vercel template instead hard-deletes the edited message and all after it (simplest; no undo). Complaints in the community threads (unconfirmed by the vendors): hidden or removed pagers (ChatGPT Feb 2026, Claude desktop Jul 2026), edit only on the last message (ChatGPT Mar 2026), **archived branches leaking into the model's context** (ChatGPT, Oct 2026, three posters), an unsent edit committed when an iOS app was suspended, Send silently deleting everything below.
- **Copy** is plain text in most clients and **drops the sources**; only LibreChat adds a numbered Citations footer. Open WebUI's fallback runs only when `navigator.clipboard` is missing, not when `writeText` rejects, so a failure shows nothing.
- **Context in a chat:** the big vendors send the history and compact it only near their window (published thresholds are around 100,000 tokens, more than our whole day), so none of that applies. For **search and RAG follow-ups** the standard is **query condensation**: one cheap model call turns "only after 2020" into a standalone query from a short window of earlier turns (LangChain, LlamaIndex, Perplexica; Open WebUI's default is the last 6 messages), skipped when the message already stands alone, with a bias towards searching when unsure. Nobody carries an earlier *answer* as evidence (NotebookLM: answers "only use data from your sources"). The decision rule of Perplexity, Consensus, Elicit and scite for "new search or answer from what is on screen" is not published.
- **Between chats:** ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini carry personal facts or summaries, scoped by Projects, with view, edit, delete, off, and a Temporary or Incognito mode; ChatGPT users report unrelated chats bleeding into new ones (forum, unverified). Their exact defaults were not read first-hand.
- **Accessibility** (WCAG 2.2; a scratch prototype built from this repository's own CSS and run with the repository's axe tags: 0 violations in light, dark and read, idle, hovered, focused and with the editor open, at 390 and 320 px): plain native buttons in a labelled group (not a toolbar), 36 px targets, shown on `:hover` and `:has(:focus-visible)` inside `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` and always on touch, space reserved so nothing shifts. Three gaps in our own harness: axe cannot see icon contrast (a pale icon passed), the "phone" check of `chat.mjs` is only a resized desktop (so `(hover: none)` is never exercised), and undersized targets can come back as axe "incomplete", which nothing reads.
- **Safety:** every shown citation is rebuilt on the server from this turn's own search (`answer.ts`), the gateway takes only a `system` and a `user` string (no client role reaches a model), and the chat has no tools. The history comes from the browser, so it can be forged: it is **data for working out what a message refers to, never evidence and never instructions**.
- **Law (not advice):** India's DPDP Act 2023 notice and consent duties are reported as due about 13 to 14 May 2027, with a MeitY proposal to bring them to 13 Nov 2026 that was not confirmed in anything the study could open. The policy in the repository is an unreviewed draft; wording is the owner's, with a lawyer.

The label must not be separable from the text outside the page. |
| A3 | **Edit-and-resend** with an inline editor (not the box), **Cancel** and one commit button, **Save and resend**; no "save without re-asking". | An unchanged answer, with its citations and strict-check badge, under changed words would break "every claim tied to this question". |
| A4 | An edit **keeps the replaced version** (text and the messages after it) as a version of that message, shown by a pager "Version 2 of 3" that is always visible when there is more than one. At most **5 versions** per message; the oldest archived one goes first. | Every client that does it well does; the research's complaints are about versions that vanish. Fits the reducer, the scroll code and the context builder, which all assume a linear active path. |
| A5 | Edit is **refused while a reply or a strict check is running** (the button says why); there is no auto-commit on blur or on the page being hidden; the draft stays in the editor if a send is refused (busy, over the limit, allowance used). | The "one message at a time" rule and the iOS incident. |

| A7 | The **reply** of an edit is a new reply with a **new id**, and the replaced messages are never sent anywhere. | An aborted run's late events must not hit the new reply. |

| B2 | The **browser** builds the history from the messages **before** the one being sent (so an edit, and Search again on an old reply, send the context that existed then, not the later turns); the **server** validates it, caps it and treats it as untrusted data. | The server keeps no chat; a body from the browser is forgeable. |
| B3 | **No history reaches the support judge, Check strictly, or the general-background call** (that one gets the standalone question only). The writer gets an earlier answer only for a "same papers" or "restyle" follow-up, as a fenced non-evidence block. | The judge is what keeps an earlier answer from becoming support. A canary test pins it. |
| B4 | A follow-up becomes a **standalone question and English search query** in the **same router call** (not a new step), validated on the server; "only after 2020" becomes a **year range applied to `Paper.year` after the search and before the cards are numbered**; any failure falls back to today's behaviour. | One call, one visible line ("I searched for: ... · Use my exact words"), and no word-level trick that could not be honoured. |
| B5 | The rules become **context-aware only when there is history**; with none, the code path and the prompts stay byte-identical to today (the 203-message router eval is untouched). | Nothing already measured moves. |
| B6 | A worker variable `CHAT_CONTEXT=off` makes the server ignore `history` **and `earlier`**: neither is read nor refused (as `CHAT_JUDGE=off` does for the judge). | C1 | **Chat to chat, by choice:** the person picks an earlier chat to continue from; a visible chip says so and can be removed. What is carried is that chat's questions and search words, **never its answers, ideas or pasted lists**. | An explicit choice: no hidden memory, nothing bleeds in that the person did not pick. |
| C2 | **Chat to chat, automatic, as a switch that is off by default** ("Use my earlier chats", in Settings, remembered on the device). When it is on, the browser picks at most 3 earlier questions on the device, shows them on the reply, and each can be removed. | **Owner's answer, 2 Nov 2026: both.** This changes the recorded decision "memory tiers: this chat and saved papers; no memory of the person" (`unified-workspace.md` 4.1), which is updated with this note. Off by default, visible, removable, and never from an Idea Check or a list, because the research found that hidden memory is where the privacy and topic-bleed problems are. |
| P1 | **The privacy text is changed and `PRIVACY_VERSION` is bumped in the same release as the context** (section 7). | **Owner's answer, 2 Nov 2026: yes.** Wording stays a draft for the owner and a lawyer. |

## 4. Part A: Copy, Edit-and-resend, versions

### Data (additive; no IndexedDB version bump, no new role, no new store)

`UserMsg` gains optional `versions?: { text: string; at: number; tail: ChatMsg[] }[]` and `ver?: number`: all versions in creation order, `ver` the active one, whose entry keeps `tail: []` because the live messages after the user message **are** its tail. A chat saved before this opens unchanged (a test next to the old-shape test in `reducer.test.ts`). `trimForStorage` and `interruptAssistant` recurse into archived tails. Pure operations in `packages/shared/src/chat-state.ts`, unit-tested in `apps/api/src/chat`: `editUserMessage(msgs, userId, text, newReply)`, `switchVersion(msgs, userId, k)`, `versionInfo`, `answerAsText`, `contextBefore` (part B), `titleAfterEdit`.

### The store (`apps/web/src/lib/chat.ts`)

- `editMessage(userId, text)` beside `sendMessage` and `retryReply`: refuses with `busy` while the chat has a run; derives mode and scope from the paired reply as `retryReply` does; refuses unchanged text; aborts strict checks and drops live Idea Check ids of the removed tail; persists at once; retitles the chat when the first message was edited.
- **Fixes that are needed whatever else is built:** `run()`'s `finally` deletes `runs[chatId]` by chat id, so a stopped run that ends after a new one began unregisters the new one: guard it by controller identity. `touch()` rebuilds the index entry from three fields: spread the old entry, so a new field survives.
- The scroll effect in `ChatView.tsx` (keyed on the message count) becomes "a user message was appended" (and the existing Idea Check case); a version switch or an in-place edit must not move the page.

### The screen

Under a user bubble, outside the accent bubble, a labelled group "Your message N": version pager (only when there is more than one), **Copy**, **Edit**. Under a finished reply: Copy answer next to Search again, Change and Check strictly. Hidden only inside `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` (opacity 0, space reserved, revealed by `:hover` of the whole row and `:has(:focus-visible)`, pinned for 1.8 s after a copy and while the editor is open, never `display: none`, never a comma list), always shown on touch. 36 px buttons, 16 px lucide icons, `--muted` on `--surface-2` (at least 5.0:1 in all three themes). Names stay the same when the state changes ("Copy your message", "Edit your message", "Copy the answer", "Previous version", "Next version"); "Message copied" and "Version 2 of 3" are said by one polite status line per row.
Copy: the writer runs synchronously in the click (Safari and Firefox need the gesture), tries `ClipboardItem` (plain text and html) or `writeText`, then a hidden textarea with `execCommand('copy')`, **also when `writeText` rejects**, then "Could not copy. Select the text and press Ctrl+C." Reuse `CiteMenu.copyText`; the three hand-copied buttons move to one shared `useCopy` over time.
Editor: replaces the bubble and its actions; the textarea holds the full text with its counter; Enter (not Shift, not while an IME is composing) sends, Escape cancels, focus goes in at the end and returns to that message's Edit button on cancel; a refusal is a `role="alert"` line and the text stays. Before commit, when later messages exist: "This replaces N later messages. You can go back to the earlier version." A live Idea Check or strict check in the tail is named in that line.

## 5. Part B: Context between messages in a chat

### Contract

`POST /api/v1/chat` gains an optional `history`, valid only with `mode: "auto"` (400 `invalid_flag` otherwise, as `anyway`). Parsed by one pure, never-throwing `readHistory` in `apps/api/src/chat/history.ts` (constants and types in `packages/shared/src/chat.ts`, like `readTighten`):
at most 6 entries (3 turns); `{role: "user", text}` (at most 300 characters, flattened and cleaned as `cleanChatMessage`) and `{role: "assistant", searchQuery?, language?, papers?: [{n, title (120), year}] (cited ones, at most 5), text? (last reply only; `[n]` marks, DOIs and links removed; at most 500)}`; at most 3,000 characters in all; wrong shape, role, order or type is **400 `invalid_history`**, not a repair; **413** when `Content-Length` is above 64 KB (and the parsed caps hold when the header is absent). Invisible controls are stripped (bidi, zero-width space, BOM, word joiner, tag characters) but **not U+200C and U+200D**, which Hindi needs. Absent history is today's request.

### What the browser sends (`contextBefore(msgs, userIndex)`, pure)

Only finished replies of searches, from the active path before the message. **Never:** Idea Check messages and the user text echoed with one, verify, paper and small-talk turns, general background, stopped, failed or interrupted replies, a user text over 300 characters (a pasted manuscript or list), anything at or after the message.

### What the server does, stage by stage

1. **Small talk**: with history, only social words stay small talk; question-shaped filler ("what is that", "really?", "are you sure") is a follow-up. No box introduction after an answer.
2. **Rules**: with history, a message is settled as a plain search only if it has at least 5 words and no dependency cue (English: it, that, they, same, also, more, again, instead, only, what about, paper N, first/second/last one...; Hinglish: uska, iska, wahi, aur, phir, pehle, dusra, isme...; Hindi equivalents); otherwise it goes to the model. A dependent message taken as standalone is a junk search; the other way costs about 1,000 tokens, so the default is the model.
3. **Router** (extended, one call, only when there is history and the message is not clearly standalone): `question` (a restatement that stands alone, at most 200 characters, in the person's language), `search_query` (English, at most 12 words), `intent` (`new`, `refine`, `same_papers`), `year_from`/`year_to` (integers 1900 to 2100), `answer_language`. Only enums and numbers act; **no model-written instruction is accepted**. Validated like `chat/rewrite.ts`: it adds no number, year, name or negation that is not in the message, the history or the titles; an invalid answer is a new search of the words as written. The history block is fenced, JSON-encoded, one line per entry and prefixed, **before** the message, which stays last in its own block. The prompt says a new topic gets no words from the old topic. **Language is decided by the rules on the current message** (not trusted from the model or the browser), inheriting the language of the last history user messages when the message is short ("aur bachon me?" detects as English).
4. **Search**: the year range is applied to `Paper.year` **before** `pickEvidencePapers` and the `papers` event (so card numbers and marks stay true); with a filter the search asks for 40 and a notice says when few papers remain. `same_papers` runs the **cached search again** with the anchor `searchQuery` the browser sent (24-hour cache, abstracts fetched by the server, retraction flags intact); without an anchor it degrades to a new search.
5. **Answer**: the writer gets the standalone `question` and `answer_language`. **Only for `same_papers`** it also gets the last answer (marks stripped, **at most 500 characters**: the browser and the reader cut there, so the 1,500 constant `MAX_SAME_PAPERS_ANSWER_CHARS` is a ceiling that is never reached) in a fenced block after the `Question:` line, introduced by "context to understand the request only, **not evidence**: never cite it or copy a claim, number or name from it unless the numbered papers below state it". A restyled answer is a **new** answer: every claim is judged against the abstracts again.
6. **Judge, Check strictly, general background**: no history (background gets the standalone question).
7. **Events**: an optional `context` on the existing `understood` event (`turns`, `intent`, `rewrote`, `yearFrom`, `yearTo`) next to `searchQuery`, shown as "Using your earlier question: ..." with **Use these exact words** (the plan said "Use my exact words"; see section 11); a year filter is a chip with a remove button. (An old open tab ignores a field it does not know; a new event type would crash its reducer.)

Two more fixes follow from it: **Search again** and **Use my exact words** on a follow-up must re-send the anchored standalone question, not the raw words ("only after 2020" alone is meaningless); the **chat route gets a body-size guard** (it has none).

### Cost and limits

Estimated +1,200 to +1,700 tokens a follow-up (a chars/4 estimate, English; Hindi and Hinglish not measured): about 5.8 answers a day instead of 6.7 at the new 3,000-character abstract limit, so the 60,000 allowance (D6) should be decided with that number in hand. Caps keep it bounded; a pre-flight estimate refuses a request that cannot fit before the model is called (the quota is checked before each call and is not atomic); the router call with history carries the ledger label `chat_route_ctx` so `/admin` shows the real cost; outcomes `followup_answered`, `followup_not_grounded`, `followup_rewrite_failed`, `followup_context_ignored`. The prompt order stays as measured (`Question:` first; 0 cached tokens were measured in R5, so no reordering for a cache nobody saw).

## 6. Part C: chat to chat

Both ways use **one** optional field, `earlier`, next to `history`: at most 3 entries `{question (200 characters), searchQuery? (English words the earlier turn used)}`, at most 800 characters in all, validated like `history` (400 `invalid_history`), Auto only, signed-in only. It is read **by the router only** (to work out what the message refers to, as the rest of the context), in its own fenced block "from the person's earlier chats". The writer, the judge, Check strictly and the general background never get `earlier` itself; **but the standalone question the router makes from it is what the writer and the background are asked, and it can carry words from it** (the privacy text says so). It never holds an answer, an Idea Check or verify text, a pasted list, a message over 300 characters, general background or small talk; the browser builds it, the server checks the shape again.

**Continue from an earlier chat** (C1; a control in the box's menu and on the chat rail): the person picks one chat; the chip "Continuing from: <title>" with a remove button stays until removed; the last 3 searched questions of that chat go as `earlier` with every message sent while the chip is on. A chat opened this way is a new chat.

**Use my earlier chats** (C2; a switch in Settings under Chats, off by default, remembered on this device like Indian sources; with storage blocked it stays off):
- Chosen **on the device**, never on the server: a small lexical index (a few words per chat, `kw?` on the chat's index entry, built when the chat is saved from its questions and search words; no embeddings, no library: a lazy chunk only if the first-load budget needs it), scored against the new message with a recency weight, English and Hinglish stop words from `route.ts`; the best 3 questions from the best chats, each only above a relevance floor (an unrelated question carries nothing). Measured on synthetic chats: 21 ms to index 1 MB, 0.1 ms a query.
- Only chats the rail can show (the privacy page promises "the thirty latest"; the rail lists 6): the switch's own line says which chats.
- **Shown on the reply**, always: "Used from your earlier chat: <question>" with a remove button that asks again without it; a per-chat exclusion ("Do not use this chat as memory") from the chat's menu, and a **private chat** that is not kept and not used.
- No memory text is written by a model: no extracted "memories". The index words come from what the person typed **and from the English search words the router made for each reply (checked by the server)**, so a Hindi question leaves English topic words in the index; they are written for every kept chat whether or not the switch was ever turned on, and a chat left out of memory keeps them.

**Before any cross-chat feature (a fix, whatever else is decided):** chats are **not tied to an account** in the browser (a session that expires without a sign-out leaves the last person's chats), so each chat entry gets the signed-in user's id; a chat of another user is ignored and removed, and the in-memory map of another tab is dropped at sign-out; the existing but unread `kept` flag is read; deleting a chat, the 30-chat limit, "Clear chats" and sign-out remove the index words too. There is nothing derived on the server.

## 7. Privacy text

Copy and Edit change nothing that is sent. **Part B does**, and these sentences in `LegalPages.tsx` (Privacy, "Your papers and ideas") would be untrue or incomplete:

1. "What you ask and the answers are kept only in your browser." stays true; add: "your earlier questions in the same conversation are sent with your next question to the same AI service".
2. "your question alone is also sent to the same AI service for a short background" becomes "your question (and, for a follow-up, what it refers to)".
3. "...only to decide which tool it belongs to and ... to put the topic into English search words" gains "and to read what a follow-up refers to".
4. "A search ... is sent, as the words you wrote" is no longer true for a follow-up: the search words may carry topic words from earlier messages; they are shown as "I searched for".

5. For chat to chat: "If you choose to continue from an earlier chat, or turn on **Use my earlier chats** in Settings (off unless you turn it on), up to three of your earlier questions, chosen on your device, are sent with a new question to the same AI service, only to read what it refers to; they are shown on the answer and you can remove them."

Plus the Settings paragraph on chats and `PRIVACY_VERSION` (`legal.ts` keeps its exact line shape: `staging-session.mjs` reads it). A bump re-shows the inline privacy card before the next upload, Idea Check or Literature scan; the chat itself is not gated today and a bump does not stop it. Whether consent is to be asked **before** history is sent is still open (the chat is not gated today and stays so unless the owner asks). **The owner decided on 2 Nov 2026 to change the text and bump the version in the same release as the context (P1); the wording below is a draft for the owner and a lawyer to read.** After the review of 3 Nov the page is more exact than items 1 to 5 (what the browser sends to our server and what an AI service reads are separate sentences; the writer and the background are asked the standalone question, which can carry words of the earlier messages; the copy of an earlier chat's questions kept in the reply that used them; a sign-in that only runs out holds the chats out of sight; the AI log's counts and the kind of call). The page is the source of truth and is pinned sentence by sentence in `apps/api/src/legal-text.test.ts`, which also reads the Settings help text and the runbook's context section.

## 8. Not done, on purpose

- **Memories written by a model** (a list of facts extracted from chats and abstracts): untrusted abstract text would be written into trusted memory, and it adds a model call over private text. If ever wanted, the person writes or confirms each item.
- **A server copy of the chats** (which would also break "kept only in your browser", the export and deletion promises, and the pinned API test), history embeddings, rolling summaries (a summary spends allowance every few turns and drops detail; at 3 turns of clipped text the raw window is cheaper and more faithful).
- **Context for the Ask-this-paper panels.** The panel's `history` is only the list of saved rows, never sent to a model. A follow-up there could read the saved rows on the server (`doc_qa`) without any forgeable field, but it needs its own query in `scale.test.ts`, a cap and a wording check. Copy moves to the shared component first; Edit there would be "ask again with these words" as a new turn.
- A real phone and a real screen reader were not tested by the studies; neither is claimed.

## 9. Tests and measures (tests first)

Pure, in `apps/api/src/chat`: editing and switching versions (nested, capped, old shape), `contextBefore` (every exclusion), `answerAsText` (a made-up citation or a mismatched paper id is refused; the label is always with general text; hostile titles), `readHistory` (forged roles, an invented assistant answer, 7 or more turns, 100 KB, zero-width and tag characters, Devanagari conjuncts kept, `[n]` marks, an Idea Check message, history with `mode: search`), the router parse (invalid enum, injected instruction, new topic must not carry old words, year range), the judge canary (no history text in the judge's `system` or `user`, signatures unchanged), a console spy that finds no history text, stubs that react to history so the browser tests run without a model. API: `chat-e2e.mjs` (413, forged history, kill switch). Browser (`chat.mjs`, plus a real `isMobile`+`hasTouch` context): reveal by hover and by keyboard focus, accessible names unchanged after Copy, full-text clipboard (including a message over 400 characters), the fallback path, edit (focus, Escape, limit, refusal keeps the text), the pager and no scroll yank, axe in light, dark and read in each state, **icon contrast asserted by hand** (axe does not see it), target size read from `incomplete`, reduced motion, and **what `/api/v1/chat` was sent** for every state of every switch.
**Measured before it is on:** a labelled follow-up set (English, Hindi, Hinglish; new search, refine, same papers, restyle, year filter, **topic switch** with the old topic's words forbidden), written as a dev and a hold-out part before tuning, run like `router-eval.mjs`; gates on intent accuracy, false "same papers", standalone-question validity, topic bleed, tokens per turn; the answer quality and the "unavailable" share on `/admin` before and after, as for the 3,000-character limit. The 203-message router eval must stay at its present result.

## 10. Order of work

## 11. As built (3 Nov 2026): what differs from the plan above

Written from the engineers' reports and from the code as it stands after the review fixes; where a line says "plan", it names what sections 3 to 10 said. Nothing here has met a real model, a real phone or a real screen reader (the stand-in model decides what a follow-up is read as in every test).

**Part A (Copy, Edit, versions)**
- `trimForStorage` and `interruptAssistant` take one reply and cannot recurse into archived tails, so `trimMessagesForStorage(msgs)` and `interruptMessages(msgs)` map every reply, at any depth, and the store uses those.
- **The cap rule:** dropping the oldest archived version first would drop the version the person has just left when they edit from the oldest one. `editUserMessage` drops the oldest of the versions kept **before** the edit, never the one just put away and never the new one. (Editing from the newest, the usual case, behaves as the plan says.)
- The edited message **keeps its id** across edits and switches (the slot, so a focused pager button is not lost); the **reply gets a new id** on every edit (A7).
- Copy is plain text only (no `ClipboardItem`, no html): `writeText`, then a hidden textarea with `execCommand('copy')` (also when `writeText` rejects), then the message. A reply's button is "Copy the answer" or, with no written answer, "Copy the papers"; none on a greeting, an offer to open another tool, a too-long reply or a reply that is not done.
- While an editor is open, Edit and the pagers of other messages are `aria-disabled` with the reason; the row is pinned for 1.8 s after the editor closes; with more than one version the row is always shown. When the allowance is used the edit is committed and the new reply says so, with the earlier version one press away. No allowance line on Save and resend.

**Part B, server**
- A history entry for a reply also carries optional `yearFrom` and `yearTo` (the range that reply was limited to), so that "the same papers" are the papers that were shown, and a `language` label that is accepted and never trusted. `ChatContextInfo` has `question` (the checked standalone question, only when `rewrote`; the browser needs it for Search again and Use these exact words), `fellBack: true` (the model's reading was not used) and `earlier` (how many earlier-chat questions a model read). `context` is sent only when a model read the turns.
- **The router does not see the text of any answer.** It reads the earlier user questions, the search words and year range of each reply, the cited titles and years, and the earlier-chat questions. Only the **writer** gets answer text, only for `same_papers`, at most 500 characters, marks, DOIs and links removed, and only when that earlier answer was grounded (the browser sends it only then). The plan was ambiguous (500 and 1,500); this is the smaller exposure.
- **The 5-word rule as changed.** The plan settled a message of 5 words or more with no cue as a plain search without a model. Measured on a hold-out written before tuning, 4 of 30 natural follow-ups were lost that way ("give me the short version", "gaon me kya hota hai", "2019 ke baad ke hi", a Hindi "cities?"). `needsContext` is now: fewer than 5 words, or any dependency cue, or 5 to 9 words with fewer than 2 words about a subject (fewer than 4 if it names a way of putting the answer: "short version", "in two lines", "chhota"); never for a text over 300 characters or with a line break (an idea, a list or a paper's text stands alone). A message that mentions the person's own text or papers is the first-turn router's.
- Sizes are bounded and not judged, except where a pasted manuscript could ride along: more than 6 entries or 3,000 characters drops the oldest whole turns (the last turn stays); a text over 500 or a title over 120 characters is cut on a whole character (`Intl.Segmenter`, so a Devanagari conjunct stays whole); more than 5 papers keeps the first 5. 400 `invalid_history` for a wrong role, order or type, a question under 2 or over 300 characters or with no letter, a year outside 1900 to 2100 or reversed, an answer text on any reply but the last. Unknown keys are ignored and never copied. U+200C and U+200D are kept (Hindi needs them).
- **The body limit is 80 KB, not 64 KB** (`MAX_CHAT_BODY_BYTES`): 20,000 Devanagari letters are 60,000 bytes, which 64 KB with a history on top refused. By `Content-Length` and by counting the bytes of a chunked body.
- `CHAT_CONTEXT=off`: both fields are neither read nor refused. The browser knows nothing of it (it still builds and sends them for a signed-in person; the chip and the switch still look active).
- A `same_papers` follow-up with no anchor (no earlier reply) is read as `refine` with the check `no_anchor`; the anchor is the last reply's `searchQuery`, else the question before it.
- The year filter applies before the cards are numbered; with a range the search asks for 40 (`YEAR_FILTER_SEARCH_LIMIT`). The line about a short or empty list has its **own notice kind `years`** (a member of the notice union in `chat.ts` and in `AssistantMsg.notices`; the first build reused `partial` and `nothing`, so "Search again" appeared beside a line that asking again could not mend). Only `partial` offers Search again.
- **Outcome names:** for a signed-in person's message that came with earlier turns, `answered` becomes `followup_answered` and `not_grounded` becomes `followup_not_grounded` (a message that stood alone and was settled by the rules counts too); `followup_rewrite_failed` when the restatement was not used or the model call failed; `followup_context_ignored` when turns were sent with the switch off. The `/admin` headline now counts the follow-ups (`chatRefusalShare`) and sets the two names that hide how they ended apart.
- **The pre-flight bar** (`FOLLOW_UP_TOKENS_ESTIMATE`, 11,000 tokens: router with turns 1,200 to 1,700, writer about 5,000, judge 3,900; chars/4, English) applies only when a model is about to read the turns. When the day cannot pay, the words are **searched as written** (as for a visitor), the papers are shown with no written answer, and the reply ends with reason `quota`. (The plan said the request is refused before any step; the first build did that, so no papers were shown either.)
- **Language:** the language of an answer is decided by rules on the current message; a message under 5 words that reads as English takes the language of the last two earlier questions when one is Hindi or Hinglish. The router's `answer_language` (en, hi, hinglish) applies only as an explicit request ("in Hindi"). The questions of **other chats** count as turns for the rules (so the first message of a chat continued from an earlier one is read with context and gets no box introduction) but never decide the language.
- Logs are counts and short names only: `chat_followup` (turns, earlier, mode, intent, rewrote, fellBack, check, years) and the warning `chat_history_refused`; failures of a message that came with turns are logged by kind only. The judge, Check strictly, `/chat/judge`, `/tighten` and the background call take no history (a canary test and a test that reads `routes.ts` pin it).
- The development route `POST /chat/route` accepts `history` and `earlier` and answers with `followUp` and `usage`; the labelled follow-up eval (97 cases, a tuned set and a hold-out written before tuning) and `router-eval.mjs --followup` are wired and **not yet run against the real model**; their gates were set before any run and may move after the first.

**Part B, browser**
- **The button on a follow-up is "Use these exact words"**, not "Use my exact words": it sits beside the question the model restated, and "my" over the model's words would not be true. It is not offered for a message about the same papers. "Use my exact words" stays on the "I searched for" line of a first-turn reply and is dropped on a reply that has the context line.
- **Search again on a follow-up** re-sends the person's raw words **with the history that existed before that message** (not the standalone question; the rules would settle the standalone question without the router, and only the router derives a year range). Pressing "Use these exact words", or the year chip's remove button, is a **search chosen by hand** (mode `search`, no history) of the words shown; a hand-chosen search that is asked again asks again what it searched.
- One retry **without history** when the server answers 400 `invalid_history` (with one console warning): otherwise a disagreement between the builder and the reader would end every follow-up of a chat in an error the person cannot mend.
- `contextBefore` skips an ineligible turn and goes on (the last 3 eligible turns), and strips invisible characters with a superset of the server's list before the 2-to-300 rule.
- **A visitor sends nothing.** `history` and `earlier` are built only for a person the page knows to be signed in (`owner` is a string; "not yet known" counts as not signed in, which sends less, never more). `chatRequestBody` takes `signedIn` as a required argument. The server ignores both for a visitor anyway.

**Part C, chat to chat**
- `scholar.earlier` holds **the account id of the person who turned the switch on**, not a bare "on": another person on the same computer finds it off. It stays through sign-out and is off when storage is blocked. For the switch (not the chip) `earlier` is sent only with a message that `needsContext`; the chip's chat sends its last 3 questions with every message of the new chat, whether or not a model will read them. The chip lives in the page only and is gone after a reload.
- **The rule for whose chats they are, as changed.** Each list entry carries `owner`, `kw` and `nomem`. The plan (and the first build) removed the chats of a person whose session had ended. That deleted a person's chats whenever the cookie (30 days, never renewed) ran out, silently. Now: **a different signed-in person removes the previous person's chats** (list, records, memory); **nobody signed in holds the owned chats** (out of sight, not used, not offered, kept in storage until the same person is back, first to go when the 30-chat list needs room); an explicit sign-out (`beginWipe`, then `clearChats`) and "Delete all" remove everything; a failed or offline check removes nothing; a chat with no owner (kept before this release, or by a visitor) is adopted by the first signed-in person who opens or touches it and is never drawn on automatically until then. The privacy page and Settings say this.
- **Two tabs.** The list is written back from storage as it is now (what a page cannot see is kept), and each record carries a revision (`idbSwap`), so a tab that was left behind takes the other tab's copy up and says so instead of writing its old copy over it. A chat still being answered when another tab signs out or deletes everything is not written back (checked on 3 Nov with a scratch harness: the record stayed absent mid-answer and after it).
- A private chat (box menu) neither draws on other chats nor is drawn on; it still uses its own earlier messages. "Only chats the rail can show" is the rail's latest six as they were when Send was pressed, minus the open chat and any left out. A chat kept before the words existed gets its `kw` the first time it is opened (so the switch finds none of a person's old chats until they open them).
- **A reply that used earlier-chat questions keeps them (at most 3 x 200 characters, with their search words) in its own record**, so the "Used from your earlier chat" line can be shown after a reload. Deleting the source chat does **not** delete that copy; deleting the chat that used them does. The privacy page and the retention row say so. Search again after "Do not use it and search again" sends only what is left **and** still allowed now (`leftOfEarlier`): a stored question is never re-sent after the switch was turned off or its chat was deleted.
- English and Hinglish stop words are copied into `chat-memory.ts` (plus a Hindi list) because `route.ts` does not export them. A pop-over's `z-index` is 80 (the phone drawer is 70).

**Fixed after review, 3 Nov:** `apps/web/src/lib/memory.ts` kept its own copy of the switch and cleared it only inside the Settings page's `storage` listener, so turning the switch **off in one tab was not seen by another tab that had turned it on earlier**, and up to three earlier questions could still be sent from it. It now reads `localStorage` on every call; the only thing kept beside it is "this person turned it off here and the removal could not be stored". Pinned by `memory-switch.test.ts` (8 cases, 4 of them red before the change).

**Known gaps, found in review and still open on 3 Nov**
- `ChatView.tsx` shows "Used from your earlier chat" only for a reply whose mode is `search`. When a model read the questions and routed the message to another tool ("check my references" with the chip on), nothing is shown, which the privacy page says in words. Dropping that guard is a one-line change that would let the sentence go.
- First-load JavaScript is 248.7 of 250 KB (CSS 24.7 of 26; HEAD before this release was 230.8 / 24.1): the next web change must first take something out ([chatview-and-trending.md](chatview-and-trending.md), C3).
- Unmeasured: the real router's follow-through on the context schema, the validity of restatements in Hindi and Hinglish, topic bleed, the real token cost per follow-up, a real phone, Safari, a screen reader.
